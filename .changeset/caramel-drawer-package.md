New package: `<caramel-drawer>` (and `<Drawer>` for React) adds a side drawer inside the player, opened from a chevron handle on the right edge, with chapters and playlist tabs, drawn in the player's dark glass in both page themes. A tab appears only when it has content, so a player without chapters or a playlist looks unchanged.
