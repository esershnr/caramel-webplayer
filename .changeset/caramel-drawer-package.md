New package: `<caramel-drawer>` (and `<Drawer>` for React) adds a side drawer inside the player, opened from a list button in the control bar (left of the settings button) and sitting above the bar, with chapters and playlist tabs, drawn in the player's dark glass in both page themes. A tab appears only when it has content, so a player without chapters or a playlist looks unchanged.
