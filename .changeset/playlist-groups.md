Playlist items accept an optional `group` name; `<caramel-playlist-panel>` renders consecutive items with the same group under a collapsible header. The panel also gains an `embedded` attribute for hosting it inside another surface, used by the new drawer package, and its thumbnails stay visible down to 14.5rem wide, with a small play glyph on items without a poster.
